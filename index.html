I am a Research Scientist at AWS AI. My research focuses on agentic post-training, including RL infrastructure and algorithms, as well as benchmark and environment curation for long-horizon tasks. More specifically, I aim to build reliable and efficient training infrastructure for agentic RL, and to design continual learning algorithms for online adaptation and recursive self-improvement.

I completed my Ph.D. in Statistics at UC San Diego, advised by Professor Danna Zhang and Professor Lily Weng, where I worked on model robustness and high-dimensional time series analysis. Before that, I received my M.S. in Statistics from the University of Pennsylvania, advised by Professor Weijie Su, focusing on high-dimensional statistics.
